The Partnership

A Czech performance system built for elite tennis meets a program on the rise.

Winning Minds (winningminds.cz) is a performance platform founded in the Czech Republic by Michal Cipro and Milan Večeřa. We combine traditional mental coaching with modern neuroscience (psychological profiling, autonomic nervous system diagnostics, stress reactivity testing, and neurocognitive training) to help elite athletes, teams, and clubs achieve peak performance.

This week, we are bringing the complete Winning Minds Performance System to Madison, working directly with the Wisconsin Badgers Women's Tennis program and Head Coach Marek Michalička, a fellow Czech, former Wisconsin standout, and Czech National Indoor Champion. After helping lead the Badgers to back-to-back NCAA Tournament appearances as an assistant coach, Marek now leads the program as head coach.

Our approach is structured, measurable, and individualized. We begin by testing, then explain the results, develop targeted training, and finally integrate those skills on the court. This is where performance matters most.

Questions

The things players usually want to ask first.

If something is not answered here, ask us directly. There is no such thing as a stupid question about this work.

Who sees my results?
You do. Your individual results stay between you and us. The coaching staff receives a team-level summary and general recommendations, never your personal data. If you want to share something from your results with your coach, that is your call, not ours.
Can I fail these tests?
No. There is no pass or fail and no ranking of the roster. We are mapping how your system works so we know where you can gain the most, fastest. A low number in one area is information, not a verdict.
Do I need to prepare anything?
Nothing. Come as you are. If you can, arrive reasonably rested and avoid a heavy caffeine hit right before your nervous system measurement, since it shifts the readings. That is all.
How much time does it take?
We work in morning and afternoon blocks and book individual slots around practice and classes, so nothing collides with your training or academic schedule. Exact slot times are posted in the schedule section during the week.
Is this therapy?
The performance work is coaching, not therapy. Milan also does therapeutic stabilization for athletes who need it, and that is always voluntary and confidential. Most of the week is about building performance, not fixing problems.
What if I would rather not do a part of it?
Tell us. Participation in any individual element is yours to decide. We would rather have you in the room and comfortable than complete a checklist.
